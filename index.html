About.

Hello.

As a Co-op at Johnson & Johnson, I contribute across dual tracks: leading user interviews to translate messy enterprise workflows into clear process maps for one initiative, while engineering frontend solutions with SAP UI5, Fiori standards, and S/4HANA architectures for another. Backed by a Computer Science background at Montclair State University, I move comfortably between talking with users to define system requirements and developing interfaces that respect technical constraints. My goal is to build digital products that are as human centered in design as they are robust in execution.
